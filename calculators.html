Engineering Assumptions & Scope Limitations

  • Continuous load based on 3-phase balanced sinusoidal operation.
  • Thermal derating strictly enforces IEC 60364-5-52 Table B.52.14 temperature & Table B.52.17 grouping factors.

Engineering Assumptions & Critical Safety Notice

  • Critical Safety Rule: Status is based on $R_g \le 1.0\ \Omega$ AND $E_m \le E_{\text{touch}}$ AND $E_s \le E_{\text{step}}$. An earthing grid is declared HAZARDOUS if touch voltage exceeds limits even if resistance is below 1Ω.
  • Calculates mesh spacing using Sverak & Koch formula assuming uniform soil model and buried conductor depth $h=0.5\text{ m}$.

Transformer Inrush & Harmonic Restraint

Models magnetizing inrush multiples, air-core inductance limiting, and calculates inrush decay time constants derived from winding impedance (%Z) per IEEE C37.91.

Protection Settings Recommendation

Governing Standards: IEEE C37.91 & IEC 60255-151

Full Load Primary Current (FLA): 279.9 A
Active Impedance Factor: 8.5% Z active
Peak Magnetizing Inrush (I_peak): 3,105.2 A (11.1x FLA)
Inrush Decay Time Constant (τ): 180 ms (9 cycles)
Recommended 2nd Harmonic Blocking (87H): 15% - 17% Threshold
Instantaneous Overcurrent (ANSI 50) Margin: PICKUP > 12x FLA SAFE

Engineering Assumptions & Scope Limitations

  • Peak inrush is mathematically coupled to transformer leakage impedance: $I_{\text{peak}} = \frac{100}{\%Z} \times k_{\text{geom}} \times k_{\text{rem}} \times \text{FLA}$.
  • Decay time constant $\tau$ is derived from the winding $X/R$ ratio ($ au = \frac{X/R}{2\pi f}$) and core saturation resistance.
  • Second-harmonic cross-blocking (ANSI 87T) must be enabled on all phases to prevent single-phase false-trips during energization.

Calculates symmetrical initial fault current ($I_k''$), peak making current ($i_p$) with dynamic $\kappa$ derived from system $X/R$ ratio, and evaluates switchgear breaking withstand.

Standard: Substation bus X/R = 10-20; Power Plant Generator bus X/R = 25-50.

Engineering Assumptions & Scope Limitations

  • Calculates symmetrical short-circuit current ($I_k''$) assuming nominal voltage factor $c = 1.10$ for high-voltage networks per IEC 60909.
  • Peak factor $\kappa$ is mathematically derived: $\kappa = 1.02 + 0.98 e^{-3 / (X/R)}$.
  • DC decaying component and circuit breaker breaking current at minimum contact parting time ($I_b$) should be validated in ETAP/CYME for high X/R installations.
