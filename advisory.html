$45M Substation Package 132/33kV Grid Interconnect

Substation Commissioning Readiness & 72-Hour Hold Point Clearance

Operational Challenge
Grid-tie hold point was stalled due to protective relay curve mismatch (IEC 60255) across the utility-interconnect boundary, unresolved CT polarity ambiguities, and DC auxiliary control bus voltage drops during trip coil energization.
Senior Technical Intervention
Executed comprehensive numerical protection coordination audits (IEC 60255 / IEEE C37.2) and power transformer differential protection setting reviews (IEEE C37.91-2021) with primary/secondary injection verifications (enforcing 0.3s discrimination margins), while evaluating switchyard earthing grid touch and step safety parameters (IEEE Std 80) as a distinct physical prerequisite separate from protection coordination; resolved 14 statutory punch-list hold points within an expedited 5-day on-site sprint.
✓ Verified Outcome: 72-hour pre-energization clearance achieved on first statutory submittal; avoided an estimated 18-day critical-path commissioning delay based on contractor standby rates.
Continuous Process Facility 6.6kV MV Motor Reliability

Medium-Voltage Motor Insulation Failure Diagnostics & dv/dt Mitigation

Operational Challenge
Recurring stator insulation dielectric puncture on 1.2MW induced draft fan motors following variable frequency drive (VFD) installation, leading to repeated unscheduled outages and motor rewinds.
Senior Technical Intervention
Performed high-frequency voltage reflection analysis, revealing steep-fronted pulse reflections (dv/dt > 1200V/μs) exceeding NEMA MG 1 Part 31 inverter-duty withstand limits, while evaluating stator winding insulation health per IEEE 43. Engineered targeted output dv/dt filter reactors and optimized PWM carrier switching frequencies.
✓ Observed Empirical Result: 14+ months of continuous, uninterrupted operation observed post-intervention without unplanned rewinds, stabilizing draft fan availability across full operating envelope.
